Plot Twist: World Deed

A land grab on one shared Earth.

Play it Source All projects

One shared Earth, cut into 300 meter tiles on a real map. You drop a pin anywhere in the world, your street or somebody else's, claim the tile under it, and build out from there. Everyone plays on the same planet, so the map fills up with other people's holdings.

You can play as a guest with no account, or sign in if you want your world to follow you between devices. There is no crypto, no NFTs, no real money and nothing for sale. The currency is a score, and the land is rows in a database.

The rule that makes it a game

Claiming land costs energy, and you get a fixed amount per day depending on your rank. That single constraint does most of the design work: money alone cannot sprawl you across the map, so somebody who checks in daily out-claims a much richer player who does not. Rent is idle. Land is not.

Covenants

When a build finishes you draft one of three permanent traits for that tile. Every card is a real tradeoff, something like double defense against a third less rent, and there is no free upgrade anywhere in the deck. Declining is permanent too, for as long as you hold the tile.

Running it for nothing

The backend is Supabase and the browser talks to Postgres directly, so there is no game process to keep alive. That is the only reason something like this costs nothing to host.

Having actual players

This is the first thing I built that strangers played, and it changed how I work on it. Most of the history is not features. It is somebody reporting that their unlocked regions were not showing up, or that the daily stipend double-claimed when they switched devices, or that the tutorial flag was not sticking. The bugs that reach people are almost never the ones I was worried about.
