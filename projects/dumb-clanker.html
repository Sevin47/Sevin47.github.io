Dumb Clanker

Write a program, set your bot loose, watch it lose in ways you did not expect.

Play it Source All projects

Every bot in the game is mechanically identical. Nothing to build, nothing to buy, no upgrade tree. The only thing that makes your bot better than anyone else is the program you wrote for it.

I wanted the whole game to be one skill. Any stat you could spend on would have given players somewhere else to look when they lost.

The chassis

One body with three things that turn independently. The hull drives, the turret aims wherever you point it, and the radar is a narrow 12 degree beam reaching 38 meters. The radar only reports a bot when the beam sweeps across one, so a script has to keep it moving or hold it on somebody deliberately.

One plan, plus interrupts

when the battle starts is your standing plan. Every other hat block is an interrupt: it takes over, runs to the end, then hands control back where it left off.

That structure exists because of a specific failure. In an earlier version any stack could hold the wheel indefinitely, and people wrote perfectly sensible scripts where two of them drove the hull opposite directions every tick. The bot sat there vibrating. Nothing in the script looked wrong, because nothing was wrong on its own.

Typed slots

Every number slot carries a unit, and only sensors measuring that unit can fill it. drive for offers times, turn offers angles, and something like "drive forward for [bots still alive] seconds" is never offered in the first place.

A starter script that loses

The script you begin with wins about two matches in ten. It shows the one pattern worth copying, which is sweep the radar and swing the gun onto whatever it finds, and it does everything else badly so there is something left to work out. Rival scripts are not readable. If you could open the bot that just beat you, the game would be over in an afternoon.
