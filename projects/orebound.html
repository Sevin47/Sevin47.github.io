Orebound

Dig through a vast pixel world to find the one golden pixel.

Play it Source All projects

You shoot balls into solid rock. They bounce, they break pixels, and every pixel you break is currency for firing more balls. Buried out in the world are rare upgrade pixels that unlock new ball types, and somewhere in all of it is a single golden one. Find that and the run is over.

The whole game is one HTML file. No build step, no dependencies, no server. You can save it and open it.

Two currencies

Pixels are mined and spent. Firing a ball costs a flat price and buys a fixed number of impacts rather than a stretch of time in the air, so you are always paying for work done. Any impacts a ball never lands are refunded when it retires.

Upgrade points cannot be bought at any price. They are buried, and digging is the only way to get them. Their density falls off the further out you go, which keeps them arriving at a steady pace instead of flooding the late game.

The balls

Seven slots, six built, one still reserved. Color is identity: the tech tree, the dock and the ball itself all share it.

BallBehavior
ProspectorThe one you start with. A plain bounce that breaks the softest rock in a single hit.
DirectionalTravels only in straight lines, drifting sideways each bounce to carve wide tunnels.
SmashDetonates on impact, hitting every pixel in a surrounding area.
PoisonPoisons pixels, and the poison erodes and spreads on its own.
LightningCrackles as it flies, arcing into nearby pixels before it ever touches them.
RushStreaks across the cavern like a shooting star and spends its impacts in seconds.

Choice of ball is not cosmetic. Measured on the same save, switching from Rush to Smash moved throughput from 601 to 1664 pixels per second.

Your cavern is your launchpad

Press to set the launch point, drag back away from your target to draw the sling, and release. The further you draw, the harder it leaves. A short click just fires outward from the center.

You can only launch into ground you have already opened, so the cavern you have dug is exactly the set of places you are allowed to fire from, and it grows as you mine. Auto-fire picks spots along the working face, so leaving it running widens the frontier instead of grinding over cleared ground.

The price is the only cap

There is no limit on how many balls you can have in flight. Every ball of a color already out there makes the next one of that color dearer, so your swarm settles wherever your income sustains it and grows only when income does.

Each ball has its own base price and its own growth rate, so the premium ones limit themselves. Rush climbs steeply and prices itself out after about a dozen. The starter climbs slowly and can fill the arena.
